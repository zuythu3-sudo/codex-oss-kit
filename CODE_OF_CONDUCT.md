# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps this project:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Focusing on what is best for maintainers and contributors
- Accepting constructive criticism

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing others' private information
- Spam, or impersonating another project or maintainer

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, commits, and issues that violate this code. Reports can go through the contact method in SECURITY.md.
